## Stationary hardware checklist — v0.1.1 revalidation pending

1. Close competing OBD clients, pair `OBDLink MX+ 36627`, verify Windows outgoing COM4 and physical adapter power. Do not use incoming COM3 by default.
2. With the vehicle safely parked in an open, well-ventilated area and ignition in the documented diagnostic position, start recording before Connect. Choose Bluetooth serial, COM4, 115200. Keep OBDwiz and other clients disconnected. Confirm ATI/STI identity, accepted benign settings and retained original initialization replies. If opening fails, verify pairing/port and explicitly retry; do not automate retries.
3. Confirm adapter status separately from ECU status, protocol description, and 0100/0120/0140 support. Note every responding ECU header; compare each independently. Unsupported oil/ambient/etc. must display Not supported.
4. Begin with RPM/coolant/load at idle. Compare controlled simultaneous or closely timed observations to OBDwiz (separate clients cannot own the same COM port at once). The previously observed ~780 RPM, 54 °C and 30% are context, not acceptance constants.
5. Check every sensor under known conditions and base units. Treat MAP as absolute pressure. Document source ECU, payload, formula, timestamps, comparison instrument, ambient conditions and discrepancies.
6. Verify Start/Stop Recording, raw bytes, CSV, redacted JSONL, replay and source selection. Before switching ignition off, Stop Recording and Stop polling (either order), then Disconnect. Confirm no new sends after Stop, a closed session and COM4 released for OBDwiz. If Conditions Not Correct is observed, confirm explicit ECU rejection, stale last values, 0 current coverage and no automatic retry. Reconnect only after checking ignition and adapter. Inspect per-sensor intervals with priorities off/on; total rate must stay capped. Loss-of-conditions regression is exercised in simulation.
7. Only after stationary checks, record a long controlled session and inspect request timing, error counts, memory and disk behavior. A passenger should operate diagnostics; the driver should not interact with the app.
8. Do not enable manufacturer reads until request semantics, evidence and user confirmation are obtained. Confirm decoder meaning against independent measurements before assigning VEHICLE_VERIFIED. No regeneration/SCR/adaptation routines are authorized.

## Historical v0.1.1 validation limits (multiframe added in v0.2)

No physical MX+, ECU or COM4 connection was opened while implementing or testing v0.1.1. The user's first-live-session analysis motivates the minimal regression fixtures, but it does not validate this patched executable on the vehicle. Bluetooth SPP, sensor accuracy, physical ignition behavior and long-drive reliability need revalidation. Synthetic failures approximate framing/errors; Windows Bluetooth drivers can fail differently. Multiframe ISO-TP, automatic raw/SQLite reconciliation, full Greek localization, iOS, CarPlay and manufacturer live queries are absent. Research reference comparison is positional, not automatic clock alignment. No uncertain regeneration signal triggers a warning.
